✍️ Word Typing Practice (Exact Answer Check)

Pinyin: rén

Meaning: human; person

📖 Reading & MCQs

On campus, Li Wen and Bai Jiayue met for the first time and continued chatting. Li Wen says: Wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén (我是中国人). Bai Jiayue says she is French and her Chinese teacher is also Chinese. Later in classroom after class, Annie sees photos on Chen Tianzhong’s phone and asks “Zhè shì shéi? (这是谁?)”. Chen Tianzhong tells her this is his girlfriend and she is Thai. At home Wang Yixue makes a video call to Wang Yifei and asks “Nǐ gōngzuò hái máng ma? (你工作还忙吗?)”. They talk about still being busy and missing each other.


1. What nationality is Li Wen?

A. Chinese
B. French

2. What question word does Annie use to ask “Who is this?”

A. 谁 (shéi)
B. 哪 (nǎ)

3. What particle is used at end for yes/no questions?

A. 吗 (ma)
B. 呢 (ne)
