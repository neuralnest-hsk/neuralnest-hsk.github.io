HSK 1 Lesson 4 Practice Page 我有两个孩子

✍️ Word Typing Practice (Exact Answer Check)

Pinyin: yǒu

Meaning: have

📖 Reading & MCQs

Dialogue 1: At home, Liu Ming and Wang Yixue were chatting. Liu Ming asks if Yifei is busy and how many students she has. Wang Yixue tells him she is very busy and she has twenty students.

Dialogue 2: Wang Yixue says: Wǒ yǒu liǎng gè gēge, nǐ ne? (我有两个哥哥，你呢?). Yang Tongle replies he doesn’t have any elder brothers. Then Wang Yixue asks “Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén? (你家有几口人?)”. Yang Tongle answers there are four people in his family: father, mother, younger sister and himself.

Dialogue3: On the street, Yang Tongle meets Wang Yixue with her son. He asks if this is her son. Wang Yixue says yes, she has two children, one son and one daughter. Her son is five years old this year and her daughter is twelve.


1. How many people are there in Yang Tongle’s family?

A. 四口人 (sì kǒu rén)
B. 三口人 (sān kǒu rén)

2. Wang Yixue’s daughter is ____ this year.

A. 十二 (shí'èr)
B. 五 (wǔ)

3. Which particle is used to ask back “What about you?” at end of sentence?

A. 呢 (ne)
B. 吗 (ma)
