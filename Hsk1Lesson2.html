📖 Reading & MCQs

Chen Tianzhong meets Bai Jiayue on campus. At first, Chen Tianzhong greets and calls her Annie by mistake. Bai Jiayue tells him her real name. Chen Tianzhong says duìbuqǐ, and Bai Jiayue replies méi guānxi. Later Li Wen meets Bai Jiayue for the first time. Li Wen says: “Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.” Bai Jiayue answers, “Rènshi nǐ wǒ yě hěn gāoxìng.” They introduce themselves and tell each other their names.


1. What does Chen Tianzhong say after he mistakes Bai Jiayue for Annie?

A. duìbuqǐ
B. méi guānxi

2. Who says “Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ”?

A. Li Wen
B. Bai Jiayue

3. What does yě mean?

A. also / too
B. very
